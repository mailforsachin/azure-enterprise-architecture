# ADR-012: Operational Resilience

## Status
Proposed reference architecture

## Context
Business platforms require continuity and recoverability.

## Decision
Define monitoring, backup, recovery and incident management controls.

## Alternatives Considered
Best-effort recovery or uniform resilience controls.

## Benefits
Clear operational accountability and recovery planning.

## Trade-offs and Limitations
Additional cost and operational complexity.

## Engineering Evidence
Conceptual architecture and modernization planning.

## Next Validation Step
Agree RTO and RPO targets and conduct recovery testing.

## Scope
Independent reference engineering and anonymized commercial architecture.
No customer approval or production deployment is asserted.
