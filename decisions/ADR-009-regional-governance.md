# ADR-009: Parameterized Regional Governance

## Status
Implemented in independent reference code

## Context
Regional requirements differ across jurisdictions.

## Decision
Use allowed-location policies with Canadian reference configurations.

## Alternatives Considered
Hardcoded or unrestricted deployment regions.

## Benefits
Reusable regional governance patterns.

## Trade-offs and Limitations
Regional policy alone does not guarantee data residency.

## Engineering Evidence
Governance templates and Canada parameter files.

## Next Validation Step
Confirm customer-specific regulatory and regional requirements.

## Scope
Independent reference engineering and anonymized commercial architecture.
No customer approval or production deployment is asserted.
